How to Play & How ML Score Prediction Works

2D Ballistics Archery & Live Multi-Linear Regression Engine

🎯 1. About the Game & Controls

Aim and shoot arrows across a dynamic wind field to hit the bullseye (up to 10.0 pts). Choose from 4 Bow Classes in the top bar: lighter bows like the Olympic Recurve (Cap: 75) have ultra-tight accuracy, while heavy bows like the Dragon Greatbow (Cap: 200) shoot much farther but have wider aim wobble.

Change Launch Angle Press ↑ / ↓ or W / S, drag on the canvas, or use the Angle slider.
Adjust Draw Power Press + / - keys, drag back on screen, or slide the Power bar.
Move Archer & Range Press ← / → or A / D, or drag the bottom-left WALK joystick.
Release Arrow Press Spacebar, click SHOOT, or release your mouse/touch drag.
🧠 2. How the Game Predicts Your Score After 4 Tries

Each bow has its own built-in Multi-Linear Regression (MLR) machine learning model that learns from your shots in real time:

• Step 1 — Calibration (First 4 Shots): When you select a bow, its status shows 0/4 Shots. As you fire 4 times with that bow, the game records your Draw Power, Launch Angle, Wind Speed, and the resulting Actual Score ($y$).
• Step 2 — Live ML Prediction (Shot 5 Onward): Right after your 4th shot, the model solves for the optimal weights ($\beta_0, \beta_1, \beta_2, \beta_3$) using physics-informed features:
Predicted Score ($\hat{y}$) = β₀ + β₁·(Ballistic Expectation) + β₂·(Angle/Loft) + β₃·(Wind Drift)
• Step 3 — Locked Prediction Comparison: As you aim your 5th+ shot, watch the live ML Predict ($\hat{y}$) score update in the top-right bar. When you shoot, the game locks that predicted score on screen until you angle your next shot, so you can easily compare the Predicted Score ($\hat{y}$) against your Actual Score ($y$)!
Tip: Click the "i" button in the top-left bar anytime to reopen this window.
